Serves 2

Truffle Baked Brie

  • 1/2 wheel brie (8 oz)
  • 1/2 tbsp truffle honey
  • 1/2 tsp truffle oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped toasted hazelnuts
  • 1/2 baguette, sliced
  1. Heat oven to 375°F.
  2. Place brie in a small baking dish and bake 10-12 minutes until soft.
  3. Drizzle with truffle honey and truffle oil; scatter hazelnuts over top.
  4. Serve immediately with warm baguette slices.
Serves 2

Steak au Poivre

  • 2 filet mignon steaks, 6-8 oz each
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 1/2 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 shallots, minced
  • 1/8 cup cognac or brandy
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard
  1. Pat steaks dry; season with salt and press cracked pepper onto both sides.
  2. Sear in hot oil 3-4 minutes per side, then add butter and baste to desired doneness. Rest.
  3. Lower heat, sauté shallots, carefully add cognac and reduce by half.
  4. Stir in cream and Dijon; simmer until glossy. Spoon over steaks.
Serves 2

Silky Pommes Purée

  • 1 lb Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3/8 cup warm heavy cream
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  1. Boil peeled potato chunks in salted water until very tender.
  2. Drain and pass through a ricer or mash until completely smooth.
  3. Fold in butter, then warm cream, a little at a time.
  4. Season and keep warm over very low heat.
Serves 2

Dark Chocolate Pots de Crème

  • 4 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 cups heavy cream
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1/8 cup sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt
  1. Heat oven to 325°F. Place four ramekins in a baking dish.
  2. Warm cream until steaming. Whisk yolks, sugar, vanilla, and salt.
  3. Slowly whisk cream into yolks, then pour over chocolate and stir smooth.
  4. Divide among ramekins. Add hot water halfway up sides; bake 25-30 minutes. Chill at least 2 hours.

Rigatoni alla Vodka

  • 1/2 lb rigatoni
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 1/2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/8 cup tomato paste
  • 1/8 cup vodka
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 3/8 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt
  1. Boil rigatoni in well-salted water; reserve 1 cup pasta water.
  2. Cook onion in butter until soft. Add garlic, tomato paste, and red pepper flakes; cook until darkened.
  3. Add vodka and reduce until nearly dry. Stir in cream and simmer gently.
  4. Toss in pasta and Parmesan, loosening with pasta water until glossy.
Serves 2

Truffle Filet & Mushrooms

  • 2 filet mignon steaks, 6 oz each
  • 6 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp fresh thyme
  • 1/2-1 tsp truffle oil
  • Salt and pepper

  2. In same pan, melt butter and brown mushrooms deeply.
  3. Add garlic and thyme for the final minute.
  4. Remove from heat and add truffle oil. Spoon mushrooms over steaks.

Spicy Tuna Crispy Rice

  • 1 cups cooked sushi rice, chilled
  • 4 oz sushi-grade tuna, diced
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1/2 tsp sriracha
  • 1/2 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • Scallions and sesame seeds
  1. Press chilled rice into a lined loaf pan about 1 inch thick; chill 30 minutes.
  2. Cut into rectangles and pan-fry in oil until crisp on both sides.

Miso-Butter Salmon

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 1 1/2 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp softened butter
  • 1/2 tbsp mirin
  • 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp sesame oil
  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Mix miso, butter, mirin, sugar, and sesame oil.
  3. Spread over salmon and roast 10-12 minutes until just cooked.
  4. Broil 1 minute for caramelized edges.

  • 1 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/8 cup warm water
  • 1 scallions, sliced

Matcha Panna Cotta

  • 1 cups heavy cream
  • 1/8 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp matcha
  • 1/2 packet unflavored gelatin
  • 1 1/2 tbsp cold water
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  1. Bloom gelatin in cold water 5 minutes.
  2. Warm cream and sugar; whisk in matcha and vanilla.
  3. Remove from heat and whisk in gelatin until dissolved.
  4. Pour into four glasses and chill at least 4 hours.

Creamy Ham Croquetas

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 cups whole milk
  • 2 1/2 oz finely chopped ham
  • 1/8 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 eggs
  • 3/4 cups breadcrumbs
  • Oil for frying
  1. Melt butter; stir in flour 1 minute. Slowly whisk in milk until thick.
  2. Fold in ham and nutmeg; chill mixture until firm.
  3. Shape into small logs. Dip in beaten eggs, then breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry at 350°F until deeply golden; drain and salt.
Serves 2

Garlic-Butter Shrimp

  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt

  3. Add shrimp and cook 1-2 minutes per side.
  4. Finish with parsley and serve with bread for the butter.

Basque Cheesecake

  • 12 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 3/4 cups heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1/4 tsp salt

  2. Beat cream cheese and sugar smooth; add eggs one at a time.
  3. Mix in cream, flour, vanilla, and salt.
  4. Bake 40-45 minutes until deeply browned and still jiggly. Cool completely.

Crispy Pork Gyoza

  • 12 gyoza wrappers
  • 4 oz ground pork
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped napa cabbage
  • 1 scallions, minced
  • 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp grated ginger
  • 1/2 tbsp neutral oil
  1. Mix pork, cabbage, scallions, soy sauce, sesame oil, and ginger.
  2. Fill wrappers with about 1 teaspoon filling; wet edges and pleat closed.
  3. Pan-fry in oil until bottoms are golden. Add 1/3 cup water, cover, and steam 4-5 minutes.
  4. Uncover and cook until bottoms crisp again.
Serves 2

Sapporo-Style Miso Ramen

  • 4 oz fresh ramen noodles
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp white or yellow miso
  • 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tbsp butter
  • 4 oz ground pork
  • 1 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 tsp grated ginger
  • 1/2 cup corn
  • 1 soft-boiled eggs, halved
  • 1 scallions, sliced
  1. Brown pork in a pot. Add garlic and ginger and cook 30 seconds.
  2. Add stock and soy sauce; simmer 10 minutes. Whisk miso with a little hot broth, then return it to the pot. Do not boil hard after adding miso.
  3. Cook noodles separately and divide between two large bowls.
  4. Ladle over broth and pork; top with butter, corn, egg, and scallions.
Serves 2

Beignets

  • 1/2 packet active dry yeast
  • 3/8 cup warm water
  • 1/8 cup sugar
  • 1/2 egg
  • 1/4 cup evaporated milk
  • 1 3/4 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp butter, softened
  • Oil for frying
  • Powdered sugar
  1. Bloom yeast in warm water with a pinch of sugar.
  2. Mix in sugar, egg, milk, flour, and butter; knead until smooth. Rise 1 hour.
  3. Roll 1/4 inch thick and cut into squares.
  4. Fry at 350°F until puffed and golden; bury in powdered sugar.

Herb Pork Tenderloin

  • 1 pork tenderloins, about 1 lb each
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 tsp thyme
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Black pepper
  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Rub pork with oil, Dijon, garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper.

Gruyère Potato Gratin

  • 1 1/4 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 cups heavy cream
  • 1 garlic cloves
  • 3/4 cups grated Gruyère
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Black pepper
  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Rub baking dish with cut garlic.
  2. Layer potatoes with salt, pepper, and Gruyère.
  3. Pour cream over top and cover with foil.
  4. Bake 50 minutes; uncover and bake 25-30 minutes until browned.

Warm Apple Cake

  • 1 apples, diced
  • 3/4 cups flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup butter, softened
  • 3/8 cup brown sugar
  • 1 eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • Vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Butter an 8-inch pan.
  2. Beat butter and sugar; add eggs. Fold in flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and milk.
  3. Fold in apples and spread into pan.
  4. Bake 35-40 minutes. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
